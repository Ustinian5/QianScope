# Simulation

Simulation is a deterministic, batched state transition over a generated homophilic graph. Control, 30% price increase, and price increase with student discount share the same initial population and seed. Each day resolves broadcast and neighbor exposure, applies bounded social influence, aggregates with survey weights, and persists a hash-verified snapshot.

`ActiveAgentSelector` assigns the top 1% to key-agent capacity, the next 10% to representative policy, and the remainder to vectorized background policy. The demo does not spend LLM calls. A configured provider may serve key agents through the typed policy adapter.
